How We Make Our Chocolate Truffle Ice Cream

How We Make Our Chocolate Truffle Ice Cream

Marcus Johnson Store Manager
June 12, 2026 6 min read Recipe

Every scoop of our chocolate truffle ice cream starts with a single bar of 70% single-origin dark chocolate and ends with a ribbon of hand-made truffle folded through a slow-churned custard. Here is exactly how we do it, from the kitchen at 123 Dessert Lane.

Start with the chocolate

We are picky about cocoa. The chocolate is melted over a gentle bain-marie, never a hot pan, until it reaches a silky 45°C. Melting too fast burns the delicate notes of the bean, and you can taste the difference in the very first bite. Once smooth, it sits covered and warm while we build the base around it.

Build the custard base

The base is a classic French custard: whole milk, heavy cream, egg yolks and a little sugar whisked over low heat until the mixture coats the back of a spoon. We stir in the warm melted chocolate and a scrape of vanilla bean paste, then press it all through a fine sieve to catch any stray bits of egg or cocoa.

“Good ice cream is twenty percent recipe and eighty percent patience. If you rush the churn, the chocolate never gets a chance to shine.”

— Marcus Johnson, Sugar & Scoop

Churn slowly, freeze fast

The chilled custard goes into our machine and churns for a good forty minutes at a lower speed than you might expect. Slow churning folds in just enough air to make the scoop light without losing any of that deep cocoa flavour. The finished batch is frozen fast and deep so the ice cream stays smooth instead of icy.

The truffle fold

The last step is our little obsession. Soft chocolate truffles are broken into ragged pieces and folded through the freshly churned ice cream by hand. The chunks stay tender at freezer temperature, so every spoonful has a patch of intense, fudgy chocolate hiding in the middle. That is the moment the flavour finally tastes like Sugar & Scoop.

Want to taste the finished result? It is scooped fresh every day in store, and we would love you to come and try a cone on us.

What you will need

  • 250g70% single-origin dark chocolate
  • 500mlWhole milk
  • 300mlHeavy cream
  • 6Large egg yolks
  • 120gFine sugar
  • 1 tspVanilla bean paste, plus a pinch of sea salt
