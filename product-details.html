Our Rainbow Sprinkle Cone starts with a Madagascar vanilla bean gelato churned in small batches every morning. Each scoop is then rolled through a blizzard of house-made rainbow sprinkles - no palm oil, just real colours from natural sources.

It lands on a waffle cone baked to order, crisp and golden with a whisper of cinnamon. Crunchy on the outside, impossibly creamy on the inside - a Sugar & Scoop classic since day one.

  • Whole milk & cream from local dairy farms
  • Madagascar vanilla bean paste
  • Cane sugar
  • House-made rainbow sprinkles (natural colours)
  • Waffle cone (wheat flour, butter, cinnamon)
  • Contains milk, wheat, gluten and egg
  • May contain traces of tree nuts, peanuts, soya and sesame
  • Nut-free and dairy-free alternatives prepared daily — ask our team
  • Full allergen chart available in-store and on request
