Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Sugar & Scoop we respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us when ordering, subscribing to our newsletter or contacting us - such as your name, email address, delivery address and order history.

How we use it

We use your information to process orders, deliver desserts, send offers you have opted into, and improve our services. We never sell your data to third parties.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep the cart and theme preferences working. Analytics cookies are only used with your consent.

Contact

For any privacy questions, email privacy@sugarandscoop.com or write to 123 Dessert Lane, New York, NY 10001.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: January 2026

Orders

All orders are subject to availability. Prices are shown in USD and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

Delivery

We deliver within the city in insulated packaging. Orders over $25 qualify for free delivery. Delivery times are estimates and may vary in peak season.

Allergens

Our parlour handles dairy, nuts, gluten and soy. Please tell our team about any allergies before ordering. Products may contain allergens.

Returns

As our products are perishable, refunds are only offered for incorrect or damaged orders reported within 2 hours of delivery.
